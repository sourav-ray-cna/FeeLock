Free trials are easy to start and easy to forget.

Nearly half

Surveys of US consumers, including C+R Research, find that nearly half of people have been charged after forgetting to cancel a free trial. Prices creep up without notice, and cancelling often means digging through menus or making a phone call.

We are building FeeLock for students and young professionals in Indian cities who juggle streaming, music, and AI tool subscriptions.

One app that watches, blocks, and cancels.

Finds every subscription

Connect Gmail or Outlook and FeeLock lists what you pay for, with each price and next billing date.

Burner cards for trials

Sign up for a trial with a card that has a hard spending cap and a short expiry, so it cannot turn into a surprise charge.

Price-hike alerts

FeeLock compares each new bill with your earlier ones and tells you when a price goes up.

Cancel in one tap

Pick a subscription you want gone and an AI agent walks through the vendor's cancellation steps for you.

How it works

  1. Connect your emailSign in with Google or Outlook. FeeLock reads receipts to find subscriptions.
  2. Review your listSee every active subscription, spot price increases, and pick what to keep.
  3. Protect and cancelCreate a burner card for your next trial, and cancel unused subscriptions with one tap.
