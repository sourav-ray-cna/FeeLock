    Questions

    Is FeeLock available to download?

    Not yet. FeeLock is a student-built concept, and this page shows simulated screens. Follow the project on GitHub to see progress.

    How would you protect my email and card data?

    We plan to use read-only sign-in with Google and Microsoft, and card issuing through a regulated banking partner. Security and compliance are our first priority before any launch.

    Will the cancellation agent work on every service?

    No. Some services need a phone call or a human reply. FeeLock will tell you when a cancellation can't be completed automatically and guide you through the steps.

    Who is FeeLock for?

    Students and young professionals in Indian cities who pay for streaming, music, and AI tools.
